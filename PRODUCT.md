# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Two distinct user groups:

**Applicants (Startups):** Founders and early-stage startup teams applying to collaborate with Audi's Innovation Hub. They're ambitious, short on time, and need to quickly understand where they stand in the process. They check the dashboard from mobile and desktop, often weeks after submitting.

**Audi Staff / Admins:** Internal reviewers and department leads who evaluate applications, route them to departments, shortlist, and make decisions. They need efficient overview of many applications.

## Product Purpose

A platform that connects innovative startups with Audi's internal teams. Startups apply via an AI-guided conversation, Audi staff review and route applications, and accepted startups get onboarded with an NDA and assigned Audi contact. Success means a startup feels valued throughout the process and Audi staff can efficiently manage high volumes of applications.

## Brand Personality

Bold, Modern, Accessible. Audi's precision meets startup energy. Not cold corporate — warm enough that a founder feels like a real partner, confident enough that Audi's quality is never in question. Voice is direct, never bureaucratic.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS cream with teal accents (Notion-style, overly soft)
- Heavy enterprise gray dashboards (SAP, Oracle)
- Startup-bro neon-on-black (Product Hunt vibes)
- Overly animated "look how modern we are" interfaces

## Design Principles

1. **Status is sacred.** The applicant's primary question is "where am I?" Every screen should answer this without hunting.
2. **Action over information.** When something is needed from the user (sign NDA, schedule meeting), make the action obvious and the explanation minimal.
3. **Earned confidence.** The UI should feel as premium as the brand without being intimidating. Startups should feel like invited guests, not applicants.
4. **Progressive disclosure.** Show the minimum needed at each status. Don't frontload every future step.
5. **Sparse but intentional.** Whitespace is premium. Every element earns its place.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Sufficient contrast ratios on the dark background. No motion for users who prefer-reduced-motion.
